What is the Difference Between an Official Transcript vs.an Unofficial Transcript-and Which One is the Best for You

  • An official transcript is considered official only when it is sent directly to the final recipient (such as a school, employer, or organization). Once it is delivered to a student or accessed by anyone other than the intended recipient, it is no longer official. If you need a transcript for a school, organization, or employment, request an official transcript and provide the recipient's correct email or mailing address.
  • An unofficial transcript contains the same academic information as an official transcript and is free to access. It is intended for personal review or record-keeping and can be viewed or printed through your student portal.

Please only complete this form if you need an official transcript. If you do not need an official transcript, you may access your unofficial transcript directly through the student portal.
